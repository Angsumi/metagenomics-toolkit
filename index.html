Metagenomics, made easier.

A command-line tool and a GUI that take you from raw sequencing reads to an annotated, reproducible report, without needing to be a full-time bioinformatician.

  • Amplicon (16S, 18S, ITS) and shotgun data
  • Built on established tools, with every version and parameter recorded
  • A plain-language report with methods text for your manuscript

Open source on release. Follow progress on GitHub.
